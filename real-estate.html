Forum

Located in the neighbourhood of Pocitos, Forum has unique views of the promenade, the sea and the central gardens from all apartments. It is the most unique structure in all of Uruguay. Designed by the Carlos Ott Arquitectos studio in association with Carlos Ponce de León Arquitectos.

Nostrum Bay

Located downtown with amazing views of Montevideo port, Nostrum Bay stands 26 stories tall. It is one of the tallest buildings in Montevideo and all of Uruguay. Designed by renowned architects Carlos Ott and Carlos Ponce de León.

View on Airbnb

Andes City

Located in the Barrio Centro, the Andes City project is one block from the main avenue of Montevideo, next to the Plaza Independencia and just steps from the Solís theater, the Sodre national auditorium and the Sala Verdi, among many other cultural centers and museums.
